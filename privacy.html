SeqScope隐私政策 · 2026-10-08

SeqScope无需注册账号。设备地址、连接配置、收藏点位、诊断报告、读取结果和连接日志存储在本机。连接密码通过系统安全存储保存。我们没有运营接收这些工业数据的后台，也不内置广告或用户行为分析服务。

局域网权限用于发现和连接你指定的设备。诊断会向目标设备发送只读协议请求。请仅连接你获准访问的网络。

SeqScope 1.0 免费提供全部功能，不含内购、订阅或广告，也不收集任何用于追踪的标识符。

只有你主动使用系统分享功能时，选定的报告、日志或报文才会交给你选择的应用或接收方。导出可能包含设备地址、业务数据和你填写的工程师、现场名称，请在分享前检查。MQTT 连接凭据在报文缓存和导出中脱敏。

你可在 App 中删除设备配置、收藏及诊断报告；删除设备同时清理对应保存的凭据。卸载 App 可删除应用容器中的本地数据；已导出的文件和系统备份需要分别管理，建议卸载前删除保存的设备以清除安全存储中的凭据。系统备份行为由你的 iOS 设置控制。

演示数据在独立内存环境中生成，不连接实际设备。退出后清除。

SeqScope Privacy Policy · 2026-10-08

No account is required. Device addresses, connection settings, saved points, diagnostic reports, readings and connection logs are stored locally. Passwords are kept in system secure storage. We do not operate a backend receiving this industrial data, and include no advertising or behavioral analytics service.

Local Network permission is used to discover and connect to devices you specify. Diagnostics send read-only protocol requests. Connect only to networks you are authorized to access.

All features in SeqScope 1.0 are provided free of charge. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no advertising, and no identifier is collected for tracking.

Only when you initiate sharing are selected reports, logs or traces passed to the app or recipient you choose. Exports may contain device addresses, business data and engineer/site names. Review them before sharing. MQTT connection credentials are redacted in the trace cache and exports.

You can delete saved devices, points and diagnostic reports in the app. Deleting a device also removes its saved credentials. Uninstalling removes local app-container data; exported files and system backups must be managed separately. Delete saved devices before uninstalling to clear credentials in secure storage. iOS settings govern system backups.

Demo data is generated in a separate in-memory environment, connects to no real devices and is discarded when you exit.
